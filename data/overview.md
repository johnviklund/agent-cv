# Overview

John Viklund is CX AI Lead at Volvo Cars and an applied-AI product leader with roughly 25 years of experience across enterprise software, product management, AI entrepreneurship, consulting, customer experience, global platforms, and transformation.

He was Co-founder, CEO, and Head of Product at Chalmers Ventures-backed Tajitsu Industries, which built AI-based predictive customer intelligence more than a decade before the generative-AI wave. Today he defines and operationalizes AI strategy across Volvo Cars Customer Experience while remaining hands-on with prompts, context, tools, evaluations, agent harnesses, automation, and AI product development.

His strongest profile is builder-leader or player-coach: senior enough to set direction and align an enterprise, but most energized when he stays close to products, architecture, experiments, and implementation.

## Career narrative

Hands-on enterprise software consultant and developer → international solution and pre-sales specialist → senior customer-communications product leader → startup product leader and CEO → Customer Experience consultant and enterprise architect → global subscription, Customer Care, CRM, and regional-transformation leader → CX AI Lead.

## Beyond the résumé

John has been curious about computers for as long as he can remember. He started exploring early IBM PCs as a child and still spends much of his free time building software, testing product ideas, and refining agentic workflows. That curiosity has remained constant as his career has moved through engineering, product management, entrepreneurship, Customer Experience, and AI leadership.

Born in Riyadh and raised across Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Uruguay, and Sweden, John is comfortable working across countries, cultures, and disciplines. Colleagues often seek him out when a situation is complicated or unclear. He connects the right people, turns scattered information into a coherent story, and helps teams agree on a practical way forward. He works best with a clear purpose, mutual trust, room to think, and a balance of focused work and close collaboration.

Outside work, John played elite youth football for Frölunda Fotboll in Pojkallsvenskan. He remains a committed Liverpool supporter, has played golf for around 20 years, enjoys cooking food from many different cuisines, and loves to travel.
